## 📖 About

This repository contains the **personal notes I create while studying for my Computer Science degree**.

I'll try to keep it updated with new notes, exercises and material throughout my entire university journey.

The goal is simple: instead of keeping everything sitting on my devices, I'd rather make it available to anyone who might find it useful.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 🇮🇹 **The notes are currently available only in Italian.**
>
> Since I'm studying my degree in Italian, my notes are naturally written in Italian as well.
> I'd love to eventually provide an English version, but unfortunately I don't have enough time to maintain both right now.
>
> Maybe someday.™ 😭

---

## 💡 Why I'm Sharing This

I'm doing my degree while also **working full-time as a software developer**.

Because of that, I know firsthand how difficult studying independently can be — finding the right resources, understanding topics on your own, doing exercises and somehow fitting everything into the little free time you have.

Sometimes it genuinely feels impossible.

That's one of the main reasons I'm making this repository public.

If my notes can save another student some time, help explain something they were struggling with, or simply make studying a little easier, then sharing them was worth it.
