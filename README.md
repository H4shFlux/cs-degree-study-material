## 📖 About

This repository contains the study material I create throughout my **Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science at the University of Pisa**.

You'll find my personal:

- 📝 Notes and summaries
- ✏️ Exercises and solutions
- 💻 Programming examples
- 🔗 Useful resources

The repository will continue to grow as I progress through my degree.

---

## 💡 Why?

I'm studying Computer Science while also working **full-time as a software developer**, which means a large part of my studying happens independently.

Finding good, structured resources isn't always easy. Useful material is often scattered across the internet, and sometimes finding the right explanation takes more time than actually studying it.

So I thought: **why keep everything I create to myself?**

My goal is to make my study material freely available to other students and anyone learning Computer Science independently.

If it makes studying even a little easier for someone else, then this repository has done its job. :)
